# Intelligentsia AI

> Bring helpful intelligence into the products people already use.

## The Opportunity

An AI capability layer that helps connected products answer questions and support knowledge-rich experiences, so intelligent assistance appears where it is useful.

## Who It Serves

People using AI-enabled services and teams creating them.

## What It Makes Possible

- Support question answering and context-aware assistance
- Enable intelligence inside participating products
- Help teams bring AI closer to the people they serve

## How People Use It

1. Open AI assistance within a participating product.
2. Ask a clear question or describe the task at hand.
3. Review the response and apply it with judgment.

## How It Connects

- Designed to complement connected products across AIWBT and the wider ecosystem.
